# FR-04: Curriculum Versioning — Retired

Curriculum version history and comparison are not part of the current product
workflow. The current curriculum is edited in place and changes take effect
immediately without drafting, approval, publishing, or creating another
curriculum version.

The relational schema may retain its existing version-scoped technical identity
for compatibility with courses, outcomes, mappings, verification, audit, and
Neo4j projection. That internal key is not a user-facing version and shall not
be exposed as a version selector, version-history screen, or approval workflow.
